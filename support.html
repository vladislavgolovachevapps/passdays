Frequently asked

How does PassDays count my days?

You can log trips manually, import them from your photos or a CSV file, or let PassDays auto-detect the country you're in from your location. It then calculates your days against each limit or residency target you've set up. An optional count-by-night mode counts the nights you actually stayed, matching how many tax and residency rules define presence.

Is my travel data private?

Your trips, limits, and preferences live on your device. There's no account and no sign-in, and we don't sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for the full details, including optional iCloud sync and analytics.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open Settings → [your Apple ID] → Subscriptions on your device to review, change, or cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I think a day count looks wrong.

Email us with the country and dates involved and we'll take a look. PassDays is a personal tracking tool, so it's always worth confirming important dates with official sources too.
